Sarah seeks to be a thorough observer, studying the relationships between contrasting ideas: depth and buoyancy, pressure and weightlessness, the serious and the ridiculous.

Curiosity and intention guide her work, grounded in reverence for the shifting tides of nature, of being human, of being in relation.

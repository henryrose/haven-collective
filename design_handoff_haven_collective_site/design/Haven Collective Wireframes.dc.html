2aMedia system — what each kind of photo is for, and how it gets cropped
1 · Room shots
install photos, candid, phone-shot, messy edges
as shot
wide crop → hero / page header
tight crop → kills clutter (bikes, cords)
Atmosphere, not documentation. Crop hard, use big and full-bleed. Lives on the event archive and as the homepage hero when there's no poster.
2 · Work
photos of individual pieces
Grids use one fixed ratio (4:5 here) so mixed-orientation uploads sit evenly; tap opens the full uncropped frame with title / year / medium. Lives on artist pages; one key work per artist feeds the home grid.
3 · People
group photo, artists hanging work
About page
Group shot anchors About. Until headshots exist, portraits are circle-crops from it. Install candids (hanging the quilts) go in the archive as "making the show".
Ask of the artists: one key-work photo each (any orientation, we crop) · a short caption per piece · a group photo in good light · install shots from every show, phone is fine · Jaimie's video as a link.
